# Code of conduct

Arc adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every Arc repository and community space.

## Scope

The code of conduct applies in all project spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
review comments, chat channels and events run by the project. It also applies when an individual
represents the project in public, for example when acting through an official account or presenting
on behalf of the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent; critique work, not people.
- Keep discussion technical and on topic; give reasons for objections.
- Welcome newcomers and people from every professional background.
- Credit the work of others accurately.
- Accept decisions made through the project's [governance](GOVERNANCE.md), including those you
  disagree with.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the
[organization profile](https://github.com/ACKaraca). Reports are handled confidentially. A
maintainer who is the subject of a report takes no part in handling it. Enforcement follows the
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
