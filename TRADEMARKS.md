# Trademark policy

The Arc source code is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The project's names and logos
are not licensed with the code. This policy states how they may be used.

## The marks

The names Arc, ArchBuilder, DraftBuilder, CivilBuilder, SolidBuilder, MeshBuilder, RenderBuilder,
Arc Review and Arc Hub, and the logos and icons that identify them, are marks of the project. The
project makes no claim that any of them is registered.

## Permitted uses

No permission is needed to:

- distribute unmodified official builds published by the project, under their own names and logos;
- refer to the project or its products factually, for example in articles, books, courses, reviews,
  comparisons, conference talks and job postings ("trained in ArchBuilder", "exports to Arc Review");
- state that your software works with Arc products or reads and writes their documents, as long as
  the statement is accurate;
- name community content that is clearly unofficial, such as user groups, tutorials and templates,
  for example "ArchBuilder tips (community)".

Every use must be accurate and must not suggest that the project endorses, sponsors or certifies you
or your work.

## Modified distributions

A build made from modified source code, or packaged differently from the official builds, must:

1. use a different name that is not confusingly similar to any of the project's names;
2. remove the project's logos and icons and replace them with your own;
3. state clearly, in its about box and documentation, that it is an unofficial build derived from Arc
   and is not supported by the project.

The statement "based on Arc" or "derived from DraftBuilder" is permitted in that context.
Distributions that only change build settings to package an unmodified release for an operating
system distribution may keep the names after agreeing on it with the suite maintainers through an
issue in `arc-suite`.

## Plug-ins and extensions

Plug-ins, extensions, scripts and node-graph packages may use a product name only to state what they
work with, in the form "<your name> for ArchBuilder". They must not use a project name as the first
word of their own name, use the project's logos, or appear to be published by the project. Official
packages are published only from repositories of the ACKaraca organization.

## Other companies' marks

Arc documents and software mention other companies' names only to identify file formats, standards
and protocols, to state interoperability or migration facts, and to describe the professional scope
of a product. Those names are trademarks of their respective owners. Their use does not imply any
affiliation with or endorsement by those owners, and Arc features are never named after another
company's features.

## Questions

Ask about a use not covered here by opening an issue in
[arc-suite](https://github.com/ACKaraca/arc-suite). The suite maintainers decide and may update this
policy through the governance process.
